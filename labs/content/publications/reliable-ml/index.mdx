## Abstract

As machine learning systems are increasingly deployed in high-stakes environments, their reliability and robustness become critical. In this paper, we propose a novel framework for evaluating the empirical robustness of deep neural networks against out-of-distribution shifts.

## Methodology

We introduce a new regularizer that encourages models to focus on causal features rather than spurious correlations. By leveraging invariant risk minimization, our approach achieves state-of-the-art performance on several challenging benchmarks.
