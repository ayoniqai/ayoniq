## Overview

The Open Bengali Language Model project aims to bridge the digital divide by creating a state-of-the-art foundation model for the Bengali language. While most modern NLP research focuses on English and high-resource languages, this project ensures that Bengali speakers have access to advanced AI capabilities.

## Key Objectives

1. **Dataset Curation**: Building the largest open, instruction-tuned dataset for Bengali.
2. **Efficient Training**: Utilizing novel low-rank adaptation techniques to train on consumer hardware.
3. **Safety & Alignment**: Ensuring the model is culturally aligned and safe for deployment in local contexts.

### Progress So Far

We have successfully curated a 50GB clean dataset and are currently in the pre-training phase. Preliminary results on zero-shot tasks show significant improvement over existing multilingual models.
